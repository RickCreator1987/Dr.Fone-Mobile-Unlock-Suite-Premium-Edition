# Prohibited Use

This software must not be used to:

- bypass iCloud Activation Lock, Google FRP, or any OEM security mechanism
- unlock a device without documented owner, enterprise, or legal authorization
- access, copy, or modify data without consent
- circumvent carrier locks or subscription locks outside official carrier processes
- violate computer misuse laws, privacy laws, DMCA, or local equivalent regulations

All device operations must go through official OEM APIs, documented service procedures,
and an authorization record with retained evidence.
